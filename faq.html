Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Motswedi SACCOS.

Membership
All employees of Botswana government ministries, departments, and parastatal organisations who are paid through the government payroll system are eligible to join. There is no minimum service period required.
Download the Membership Application Form from our Membership page, complete it, and submit it in person at Zambezi Towers, 5th Floor, CBD Gaborone with a copy of your national ID and latest payslip. Processing takes 5–7 working days.
The minimum monthly savings contribution is P50, deducted directly from your salary through the government payroll system. You can choose to contribute more to build your savings faster.
Loans
Members qualify for loans of up to four times their accumulated savings. For example, if you have saved P10,000, you can apply for a loan of up to P40,000, subject to your repayment capacity.
Motswedi SACCOS offers competitive interest rates significantly lower than commercial banks. The exact rate will be confirmed upon application. Use our Loan Calculator under Services for an estimate.
Loan applications are typically processed within 5–10 working days, provided all required documentation is submitted correctly. Emergency loans may be processed faster at the discretion of management.
Savings & Accounts
Monthly statements are sent via email to registered members. You can also contact the office directly at +267 390 8227 to request your current savings balance, or visit in person during office hours.
Members who resign from government service retain their membership as long as any outstanding loans are being serviced. Savings can be maintained or withdrawn subject to the society's rules. Contact the office for guidance specific to your situation.
