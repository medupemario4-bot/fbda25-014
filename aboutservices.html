Who We Are

Serving Botswana's public servants since 1985.

Our History

Motswedi SACCOS was established on 19 November 1985 by staff of the Department for Co-operative Development and the Botswana Co-operative Bank. The name "Motswedi" means water fountain in Setswana — a source of sustenance for the community.

Over four decades, the society has grown from a small departmental cooperative to a society serving over 3,500 members across all government ministries, departments, and parastatal organisations in Botswana.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To promote a culture of savings and provide affordable credit to members, improving their financial wellbeing.

Vision: To be the cooperative of choice for all Botswana public servants.

Our Services

Savings, loans, and share capital — built for public servants.

Savings Accounts

Contribute monthly via payroll deduction. Earn competitive interest on your accumulated savings. Multiple product tiers available based on your contribution level.

Loan Products

Borrow up to 4x your savings. Personal loans and emergency loans available. Competitive interest rates lower than commercial banks, with straightforward repayment terms.

Share Capital

As a member-owned cooperative, you hold shares in Motswedi SACCOS. Shares grow in value and entitle you to a share of annual dividends declared at AGM.

Membership

Join in three simple steps. Open to all government and parastatal employees.

Step 1 — Check Eligibility

You must be employed by a Botswana government ministry, department, or parastatal organisation and paid through the government payroll system.

Step 2 — Download & Complete Form

Download the membership application form below, complete it with your personal and employment details, and attach a copy of your national ID and payslip.

Step 3 — Submit at the Office

Bring your completed form to Zambezi Towers, 5th Floor, CBD Gaborone, Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00. Processing takes 5–7 working days.
